# MockMate promotional assets

Ready-to-use campaign assets for MockMate. The full content research and production prompt is in [`docs/MARKETING_CONTENT_PROMPT.md`](../docs/MARKETING_CONTENT_PROMPT.md).

## Files

- `mockmate-promotional-video.mp4` — vertical 9:16, 1080 × 1920, 26.9 seconds; H.264/AAC, generated narration, and burned-in captions.
- `mockmate-promo-portrait.png` — 4:5 social image, 1080 × 1350.
- `mockmate-promo-square.png` — square social image, 1080 × 1080.
- `mockmate-voiceover.mp3` — standalone narration.
- `mockmate-video-captions.srt` — timed captions for republishing or editing.
- `mockmate-video-transcript.txt` — voiceover transcript.
- `mockmate-post-copy.txt` — suggested caption, hashtags, alt text, and publishing note.
- `MockMate-Promo-Pack.zip` — the deliverables above bundled for convenient download.

## Publishing notes

The people shown are illustrative AI-generated campaign visuals, not customers or testimonials. Verify current product setup, download links, feature availability, and platform policies before publishing. The video focuses on Solo Practice and does not show a fabricated product interface. Nothing in this folder is automatically published.
