FAQ

The questions people should ask before downloading.

Do I need an API key?

Not always. MockMate supports managed services where they are enabled, and it also supports bring-your-own-key mode for supported providers. Availability and quotas depend on the deployed environment and plan.

Does MockMate guarantee the overlay is hidden from screen sharing?

No. On supported Windows and macOS environments, OS-level content protection can exclude the overlay from common capture paths. Always check the actual meeting-app share preview first. Linux does not support this protection.

What happens to my data in BYOK mode?

Your supported provider credentials are stored locally by the application. Relevant prompts, audio, screenshots, or other inputs can still be sent to the AI/transcription providers you configure. MockMate should not be described as fully offline or zero-data-sharing.

Which desktop download should I use?

The current automated public v1.5.2 release artifact is Windows NSIS. macOS and Linux runtime/packaging support remains in the repository; check the Releases page for the actual artifacts available for your platform.

Can MockMate invent achievements for my resume or interview answers?

It should not. Resume and interview workflows are intended to ground suggestions in your supplied experience and context. You are responsible for keeping every claim truthful and for following interviewer/employer policy.

Does MockMate work for coding interviews?

Yes. The desktop workflow includes coding assistance, complexity and edge-case guidance, and F7 screen-question analysis. Exact provider/model capabilities depend on your configured setup.
